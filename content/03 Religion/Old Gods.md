Though they have fallen silent in the many years since the [[First Cataclysm]], there once existed a variety of deities worshipped by the few intelligent species that now inhabit the Dead City. Their following has reduced dramatically in the present day, but the devout claim that these ancient gods still watch over their chosen few.
### Divine Spellcasting

Clerics, Druids, and some Warlocks may claim that a specific divine entity is the source of their power. While their spellcasting power is self-evident, the source of that power has never been verified by anyone but the casters themselves.
