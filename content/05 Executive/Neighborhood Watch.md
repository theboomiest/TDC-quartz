A coalition of petty criminals, outcasts, and occasional wanton do-gooders under the heavy thumb of [[One-Eyed Sloan]]. The Watch provides protection to the settlements that can afford their services.

Surprisingly disciplined given the dodgy backgrounds of most its members, the Watch's reputation keeps most raiders away. Meanwhile Sloan's reputation for ruthless justice keeps the watchmen from "relapsing" into their old ways.

Many outside observers note that the settlements which refuse the Watch's service or stop paying often find themselves under siege shortly after. Sloan vehemently denies the insinuations - "The city is a dangerous place. Do not be surprised when danger finds you."
