A small settlement built from the ruins of an enclosed trainyard. Named after a weathered sign hanging just outside the facility with only a few remaining legible letters. Ailcross is well protected from raids given the long sight lines provided by the railroad tracks, making this a favorite meeting spot for travelers and traders.

## Notable Persons

* [[Old NaN]] - keeps a salvage shop open for trade. Stocks a variety of goods that might prove useful to the average adventurer.
