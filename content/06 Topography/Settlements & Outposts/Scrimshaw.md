Fishing village on the edge of [[Lantern Lake]]. Most buildings reside on the northwestern shore, but several fishing huts reach eastward into the lake itself, hundreds of feet past the waterline. 

Columns made of steel and concrete extend above the greasy black water that support small shack houses in which the fishermen live. Ropes cross between the poles that allow precarious travel between the shacks and hold up the fishermen's nets. Boats transport goods between the mainland buildings and the fishing huts. 

Other trawlers take their boats further into the lake to scrape bits of grease and oil from the surface; careful to leave enough on top to avoid garnering attention from anything that might be watching from below. These raw resources can be refined into mechanical lubricant and lantern oil back at shore.
